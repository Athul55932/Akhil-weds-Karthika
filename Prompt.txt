### Application Structure & Page Flow

#### Section 01: Opening / Loading Screen Overlay
- **Layout:** Fullscreen modal with mask reveal animation. Warm cream surface with floating botanical SVG doodles.
- **Content:**
  - Sub-heading: *"With the blessings of our families"*
  - Main Heading: **AKHIL & KARTHIKA**
  - Sub-text: **24 · 01 · 2027**
  - Interactive Action: `OPEN INVITATION →` button styled with maroon fill and rounded pill edges.
- **Behavior:** Clicking the button triggers a Framer Motion curtain/mask fade-out reveal, plays background ambient music (if allowed), and un-locks page scrolling to reveal Section 02.

#### Sticky Navigation & Floating Music Controller
- **Navbar:** Appears after opening transition. Contains logo **"A & K"**, section links (*Our Story*, *Details*, *Gallery*, *RSVP*), and a high-contrast `RSVP` CTA button. Smooth scroll targeting.
- **Floating Music Player:** Bottom-right corner icon button with sound wave animation or subtle spin effect. Toggles play/pause state of an audio track (use a reliable royalty-free ambient wedding instrumental MP3 URL).

#### Section 02: Hero Section
- **Layout:** Asymmetric editorial grid layout. Left column features hand-drawn style handwritten text *"Together forever"*, couple names in large serif display font, wedding date details, venue preview, and a secondary action button (`Our Journey ↓`). Right column features a large organic curved picture frame enclosing the main couple portrait with SVG floral overlays.

#### Section 03: Live Countdown Counter
- **Heading:** `THE COUNTDOWN BEGINS` flanked by decorative line-art flowers.
- **Countdown Engine:** Live JavaScript dynamic target set to `January 24, 2027 11:00:00 GMT+0530`.
- **Display Grid:** 4 columns: **Days**, **Hours**, **Minutes**, **Seconds** enclosed in delicate cream panels with thin maroon border accents.

#### Section 04: Wedding Event Details
- **Heading:** `THE WEDDING`
- **Data Details:**
  - **Date:** 24 January 2027
  - **Time:** 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM
  - **Venue:** Prince Convention Centre, Alappuzha, Kerala
- **Card Design:** Left panel with event text and Google Maps direct link button (`View Location →`). Right panel displaying a polaroid/taped photo card showing the Kerala destination landscape with handwritten accent text: *"See you there! ♡"*.

#### Section 05: Asymmetric Photo Gallery
- **Heading:** `OUR MOMENTS`
- **Layout:** Asymmetric staggered grid layout (not a plain square grid). Incorporate slight photo rotations (e.g., `-2deg`, `3deg`), tape sticker corner details, overlapping borders, and scroll-triggered fade-and-scale Framer Motion effects. Include minimal handwritten captions under select photos (e.g., *"Better together"*, *"A new chapter"*).

#### Section 06: Love Quote Pause
- **Design:** Clean typographic section with ample breathing room.
- **Content:** Large quotation marks around: *"Two hearts, one beautiful beginning."* surrounded by subtle drifting petal/leaf vector graphics.

#### Section 07: RSVP Section (Frontend Only)
- **Heading:** `WE WOULD LOVE TO CELEBRATE WITH YOU`
- **Form UI:**
  - Input: **Your Name** (Text input)
  - Radio Selection: **Will you be attending?** (Option 1: *Joyfully Accept*, Option 2: *Regretfully Decline*)
  - **Note:** Exclude any number-of-guests field as per blueprint.
  - CTA: `Submit RSVP →` button.
- **Interactivity:** On submit, prevent default reload, trigger standard form validation, and switch state to display a warm visual thank-you confirmation message inside the card.

#### Section 08: Thank You / Closing Banner
- **Layout:** Full-width cinematic banner image overlayed with a deep maroon gradient filter.
- **Content:**
  - Heading: **THANK YOU FOR BEING PART OF OUR SPECIAL DAY**
  - Sub-heading: **AKHIL & KARTHIKA**
  - Date: **24 · 01 · 2027**
